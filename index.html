Frequently asked questions

Our service teams take calls Monday to Sunday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm.

780-436-5006
What methods of payment do you accept?

We accept Visa, MasterCard, cheque, or EFTs.

What are your office working hours?

We maintain a virtual office in order to minimize our costs. Our service teams can take your calls from Monday to Sunday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm. If you leave a message we will endeavour to respond as quickly as possible.

Are you insured?

Yes. We have comprehensive public liability coverage. Our insurance policy will cover any accidental damages or theft on your property caused or perpetrated by our cleaners.

Will I have the same cleaning person each visit?

Yes, you will have the same cleaning professional for each visit. We know how important it is to have someone you know and trust cleaning your business or workspace.

Do you furnish the cleaning supplies?

Yes, we furnish all cleaning supplies and equipment. Disposable items such as toilet paper and hand soap can be provided at our cost plus 15%.

What is your service area?

We provide commercial cleaning services in Edmonton and the surrounding area.
